Catering Inquiries

What is a Mini Buffet?
A Mini Buffet is a compact meal set suitable for small groups, delivered in disposable trays without warmers or setup.
Are cutleries provided?
Yes, disposable cutlery sets are provided based on your pax size.
Do you remove the trash from the venue?
For regular buffets with setup, our staff will assist in basic cleanup including trash bag collection.

Ordering Payment

How can I place an order?
Orders can be made online via our website or by calling our hotline. You will receive an email confirmation after placing your order.
Can I make any last minute changes?
Last-minute changes are subject to availability. We recommend notifying us at least 48 hours in advance.
How do I know if my order is processed?
You’ll receive a confirmation email once your order is confirmed. If you don’t see it, check your spam folder or contact us.

Delivery

Do you deliver on Public Holidays?
Yes, we do! However, slots may be limited and surcharges may apply. Book early to secure your slot.
What is your earliest delivery time?
Our deliveries typically start from 7:30 AM. Please check with us for exact timings based on your order type.
Can I extend the collection time?
Yes, you may request an extension but it's subject to approval. Please notify us at least 1 day before delivery.
